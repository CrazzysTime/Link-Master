Earth's nature:

  • Earth's nature is full of balance, where forests, rivers and oceans create a home for living beings.

  • The air we breathe, the water we drink, and the soil that grows our food are all gifts from nature.

  • Forests act like the planet's lungs, producing oxygen and giving shelter to wildlife.


  • Nature constantly changes through seasons, showing us cycles of growth, rest, and renewal.

  • Oceans cover most of Earth, regulating its climate and supporting a wide variety of life.

Mercury's nature:

  • Mercury is the smallest planet in our solar system and closest to the Sun.

  • Its surface is rocky and covered with craters, like Earth's Moon.

  • Days on Mercury are extremely hot, but nights become freezing cold.

  • The planet has almost no atmosphere, so it cannot trap heat or protect from meteors.

  • Mercury moves around the Sun faster than any other planet.

Venus nature:

  • Venus is the second planet from the Sun and almost the same size as Earth.

  • It is covered with thick clouds made of poisonous gases.

  • These clouds trap heat, making Venus the hottest planet in the solar system.

  • Its surface is rocky, with mountains and many volcanoes.

  • A day on Venus is longer than a year there, because it spins very slowly.
