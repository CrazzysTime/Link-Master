France: Paris

  • Paris is the capital city of France.

  • It is called the “City of Light” because of its beauty and history.

  • The Eiffel Tower is the most famous landmark in Paris.

  • Paris is known for its art museums, especially the Louvre, which holds the Mona Lisa.

  • The River Seine runs through the city and gives it a romantic charm.

  • Paris is also famous for its delicious food, like croissants and baguettes.

  • Paris is one of the most visited cities in the world.

USA: New york

  • New York City is the largest city in the United States.

  • It is often called “The Big Apple.”

  • The Statue of Liberty is one of its most famous landmarks.

  • New York is known for its tall skyscrapers, especially the Empire State Building.

  • Times Square is bright with lights and full of people all day and night.

  • Millions of tourists visit New York every year.

  • The city is a center for culture, fashion, and business.

Hungary: Budapest

  • Budapest is the capital city of Hungary.

  • The "Duna" River flows through the middle of the city.

  • It is famous for the Chain Bridge and the Parliament Building.

  • Budapest is known for its beautiful thermal baths.

  • The city has two parts, Buda and Pest, joined together.

UAE: Dubai

  • Dubai is a modern city in the United Arab Emirates.

  • It is famous for the Burj Khalifa, the tallest building in the world.

  • The city is known for luxury shopping and futuristic architecture.

  • Tourists love the Palm Jumeirah, a man-made island shaped like a palm tree.

  • Dubai also has a rich desert culture with camel rides and traditional souks.
