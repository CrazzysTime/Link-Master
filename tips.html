Tips of how to study better.

  • Set Specific Goals. Focus on what exactly you want to learn each session.

  • Use Active Recall. Test yourself instead of just rereading.

  • Break It Up. Study in short bursts (25-50 minutes) with small breaks.

  • Eliminate Distractions. Keep your phone and notifications away.

  • Teach Someone Else. Explaining concepts reinforces your understanding.

  • Mix Subjects. Switch topics to improve retention (interleaving).
